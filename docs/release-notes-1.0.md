# GABBERKIX 1.0

The first release: a hardcore kick designer for the M-VAVE FM-1.

- **The kick in three layers:** a synthesized 909 (sweep, hold, decay, click, noise) through EQ → DIST1 → EQ2 → DIST2
  → POST for character; a punch for the attack (the first milliseconds of this kick or a preset's, with its own EQ);
  group A pressing the two into one kick in a brickwall clipper; a sub split off group A's lows; TOTAL's EQ and
  clipper over everything. PERF's three peaks and ENV's volume shape finish it.
- **The randomizer (ARP):** a new gabber kick each press, as loud as a new one, with undo (SEL + ARP).
- **Sixteen presets** on the white keys (SAVE + key, SEL + key), and **RESAMPLE**: a preset as the source of a new
  kick, its chain blank to build on.
- **Playing:** PLAY on every beat (60–300 BPM), the keys pitched, MIDI notes in, TRANSPOSE ±12 (ALGO).
- **MIDI:** clock in (followed) and out, Start/Stop, everything passed on; USB, the TRS jack, Bluetooth MIDI, and the
  connections page (SEL + REC) for Bluetooth MIDI devices and the USB port as host.
- **The render page** ([ejtb76.github.io/gabberkix/render](https://ejtb76.github.io/gabberkix/render/)): the presets
  read over USB and rendered by the firmware's own engine to WAV or AIFF, one by one or all in a ZIP; and a backup
  of the presets.

Identity `FM-1_818010000` (pattern `FM-1_818XXYYZZ`). Every sound is synthesized: the firmware carries no samples.
Another firmware may erase the presets: keep a backup file first.
