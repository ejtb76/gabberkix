# MIDI: ours out (PLAY: Start and 24 pulses a beat, Stop), an outside clock followed (USB and Bluetooth: Start arms,
# the first pulse is beat 1, a kick every 24), ours again when it stops without a Stop; THRU; TRANSPOSE (ALGO).
master 4096
wait 300
# 160 BPM: 64 pulses a second; 1.5 s from PLAY (and its tap): 96 to 104, and kicks at 0, 375, 750, 1125, 1500 ms
tap PLAY
wait 1500
expect clocks_out >93
expect clocks_out <105
expect kicks >3
expect kicks <6
tap PLAY
expect playing 0
wait 300
# a computer's clock over USB (21 ms a pulse: 119 BPM): Start, 25 pulses: beat 1 and beat 2
midi FA 00 00
clock 25
expect playing 1
expect ext 1
expect kicks >6
midi FC 00 00
wait 20
expect playing 0
wait 600
# over Bluetooth: at the sender's time (ble_midi_time); the first pulse is beat 1
ble connect
wait 1200
midi BLE FA 00 00
midi BLE F8 00 00
wait 20
expect playing 1
midi BLE FC 00 00
wait 20
expect playing 0
wait 600
# the outside clock stops without a Stop: after half a second ours plays on (and sends nothing: no Start of its own)
midi FA 00 00
clock 30
wait 1500
expect playing 1
expect ext 0
midi FC 00 00
wait 20
expect playing 0
wait 600
# THRU: a note from USB goes on to Bluetooth, not back to USB
out
midi 90 30 64
wait 10
expect_out BLE 90 30 64
# TRANSPOSE: ALGO 3 up; PLAY's kick and the keys move, a MIDI note does not
turn ALGO 3
expect xpose 3
tap PLAY
wait 100
expect semis 3
tap PLAY
key 14 down
wait 50
key 14 up
expect semis 3
midi 90 43 64
